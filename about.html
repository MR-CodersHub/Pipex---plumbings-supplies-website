Behind Pipex

A Legacy Built on Quality Plumbing Supplies

Founded in 2011, Pipex began as a local trade counter serving neighborhood plumbers. Today, we operate a 15,000 sq.ft. distribution hub housing over 12,000 active SKUs across premier certified manufacturers.

We specialize strictly in the supply of high-grade plumbing materials and sanitary fittings—ensuring every project, from small residential bathroom upgrades to multi-story housing developments, is supplied with leak-proof, long-lasting components.
